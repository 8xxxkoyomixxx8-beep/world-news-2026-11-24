One image that sums up the world news of Tuesday 24 November 2026, drawn as an editorial front page: oil tankers striking mines in the Strait of Hormuz as Iran refuses talks until its seven conditions are met, as the lead story, with a Russian strike on a Zaporizhzhia apartment building killing 2 and wounding 40, an Israeli strike killing Hamas' internal security chief Naeem Abu Nahel in Gaza, the collapse of the COP31 climate talks without a fossil-fuel deal, Sanofi's $41bn takeover of Insmed, fourteen countries backing the ICJ's Rohingya ruling as Malaysia prepares to return 6,500 refugees, Medicare's negotiated 45% price cuts on 13 costly drugs, a magnitude 5.6 earthquake in Nepal, and a ticker of the day's smaller stories. draw.py renders the page with PIL from the day's verified headlines; main.py validates the finished PNG (signature, dimensions, size, chunk CRCs) and copies it to the output directory, byte for byte, so repeated runs land on exactly the same file.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
# writes entry.png to /output, or to $MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR when that is set
```
